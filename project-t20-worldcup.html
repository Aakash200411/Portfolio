T20 World Cup 2024 analysis

A data-driven Dream Team selection from T20 World Cup 2024 ball-by-ball and player data, plus an interactive Streamlit dashboard.

Repository Live dashboard

Overview

A side project born out of watching the tournament: instead of picking a fantasy team by reputation, I used batting and bowling summaries from every match to rank players statistically — openers by boundary percentage, bowlers and all-rounders by their own performance metrics — and assembled an 11-player squad plus reserves from the results.

Result

The final data-driven XI: Travis Head, Rohit Sharma, Nicholas Pooran, Harry Brook, Marcus Stoinis, Hardik Pandya, Roston Chase, Axar Patel, Jasprit Bumrah, Trent Boult, and Fazalhaq Farooqi — with four reserves selected the same way.

The full workflow, plus an interactive dashboard for exploring player stats, is in the linked Streamlit app.
