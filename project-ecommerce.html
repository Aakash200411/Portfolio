E-commerce sales & margin analysis

An exploratory analysis of 9,994 Superstore transactions, looking past revenue to find where the business was actually making money.

Repository

Overview

Sales figures alone can be misleading — a category can look like a top performer by revenue while barely turning a profit. I grouped the dataset by product category and compared sales against profit directly, rather than reporting them as two separate charts.

Key finding

Furniture2.5%
Office supplies17.0%
Technology17.4%

Furniture generated $742K in sales — close to Technology's $836K — but converted only 2.5% of that to profit, against Technology's 17.4% margin.

Recommendation

A revenue-only view rates Furniture as a strong category. The margin view says otherwise: it's likely being discounted aggressively to move volume, and a pricing or discount-policy review would do more for the bottom line than promoting it further.

What I'd add next

Breaking this down to the sub-category level would show whether the margin problem is a couple of specific products or the whole Furniture line.
